# Training-Time Evidence Review

This review explains evidence relevant to the user's chosen training time. The exact time is personal policy, not a universal recommendation or a rule for other deployments.

## Current schedule

- Strength: Monday and Thursday, start at 07:00 America/Chicago and finish after the complete prescription; there is no session-duration limit.

This time defines the fixed schedule within evidence-compatible boundaries. It is not presented as a universally optimal clock time.

## Morning resistance training

The 2019 systematic review/meta-analysis of time-of-day-specific resistance training found that muscle size and strength can improve with morning or evening training. Baseline performance often favors later hours, while testing gains tend to be largest near the habitually trained time [Grgic et al., 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30704301/). A later systematic review/meta-analysis likewise found no clear universal best time for health or performance adaptation [Grgic et al., 2023; DOI `10.1186/s40798-023-00577-5`].

The defensible conclusion is consistency and adherence, not an evening mandate. A fixed 07:00 start on both strength days reduces schedule variation and makes repeated strength observations more comparable.

The ledger records each session's actual start and end when known, so a session held at another time remains an honest observation rather than a schedule violation.
