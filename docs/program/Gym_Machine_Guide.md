## Approximate vintage

- D-200/300/400/600: mid-1990s product family. The D-300 cover and D-200 parts list are dated July 1996. A serial plate is still required to date each installed unit.
- CL-2403: early-to-mid-2000s design, documented by 2000 and present in HOIST's 2005 catalog. Exact unit date requires its serial plate.
- Concept2 Model D/PM3: most likely 2003–2014 unless the monitor was replaced.

The missing pound numbers on the D-series stacks fit their period and design: the manuals specify stack components, but the machine's cam/cable output is not a simple printed-pound ladder.

## Setup rules

Height supports one conclusion: begin near the middle of the geometry range. It cannot determine the correct numbered hole. Limb and torso proportions, mobility, pad thickness, adjustment numbering, and the exercise path decide the final setting.

### CL-2403 leg press and calf press

- Begin with backrest and seat slide near their midpoints.
- For leg press, adjust until the starting knee angle is near 90 degrees with the pelvis and back fully supported. Feet are around shoulder width and knees track toes.
- If the pelvis curls at the bottom, reduce depth or move the seat away. Do not chase a deeper range by losing support.
- For calf press, secure the forefoot on the plate, keep knees softly extended and still, and use only comfortable ankle travel.
- Save the back and seat-slide marks. The two exercises may require the same seat geometry, but record what is actually observed rather than assuming.

### D-200 close-underhand front pulldown

- Start with the seat near the middle; the machine decal says lower for taller users and higher for shorter users.
- Set thigh pads snugly without painful compression.
- The observed unit has a cable attachment point. Clip on the long lat-pulldown bar; do not use the rope or triangle handle.
- Hold the long bar underhand with palms toward you and hands just inside shoulder width. Lean back about 30 degrees, pull toward the upper chest, and avoid a behind-neck path.
- At full reach, remain in the seat. Save seat and thigh-pad marks.

### D-200 chest-supported mid-row

- Set seat height so the handles meet the lower-to-mid sternum in the cable line.
- Set chest-pad distance to allow shoulder-blade reach without the torso leaving the pad and without pulling the elbows excessively behind the body.
- Save seat and chest-pad marks independently from the pulldown settings.

### D-300 horizontal chest press

- Start with seat and back pad near their midpoints.
- Set seat height so handles travel across the mid-sternum.
- Set the back so the start supplies a mild chest stretch while shoulders remain supported.
- Use the horizontal press orientation and save seat/back marks.

### D-400 seated leg curl

- Align the knee with the swing-arm pivot.
- Place the moving roller against the lower calf and make the thigh restraint snug.
- Retain a slight knee bend at the start instead of hyperextending.
- Keep feet neutral. Save seat, thigh, and range marks present on the actual unit.

### D-600 lower back and abdominal

- Set the footrest and pads so the body cannot slide as resistance begins.
- Select a conservative start/range mark and test a pain-free arc before load calibration.
- Lower-back work stops before end-range hyperextension; abdominal work uses trunk flexion rather than arm pulling.
- Record geometry separately for the two machine functions.

### Back-supported dumbbell overhead press

- Use a high bench back and a neutral grip.
- Select the bench angle and seat position that keep ribs/pelvis stable and permit a pain-free bottom position.
- Record bench marks as setup facts. Dumbbell pounds are per hand.

### Timed static neck extension and flexion

- Bring one dedicated yoga block and use the same adjustable bench for both movements at 45 degrees.
- For extension, lie face-up with the block centered under the back of the head and press backward while the neutral head and torso remain still.
- For flexion, lie face-down, support body weight through the arms, center the forehead on the block, and press into it while the neutral head and torso remain still.
- Record the bench angle and yoga-block placement as setup facts. Do not infer force, pounds, or RIR from the 90-second contraction.

## Conservative load calibration

For D-200 pulldown, D-300 press, D-200 row, D-400 curl, both D-600 movements, and the two CL-2403 uses:

1. Establish geometry before load.
2. At position 1, perform 3 clean repetitions using the prescribed 5-second positive and 5-second negative.
3. If the probe is plainly easy and at least 5 additional clean repetitions clearly remain, rest 90 seconds, move one position heavier, and perform another 3-repetition probe.
4. Continue only while each test is plainly easy and technically identical.
5. Stop before grinding, rest 2 minutes, and use a conservative tested position expected to permit 5–8 clean repetitions near the target effort.

The ladder is one-time load calibration, not a recurring preliminary sequence, max test, or source of work-set records. Body weight and height do not improve this estimate.
