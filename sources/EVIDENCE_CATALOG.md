# Scientific evidence catalog

This catalog identifies the full texts used in the audits. DOI links are the public canonical references; local availability depends on the supplied private sources. The list is a citation record, not an instruction to obtain files or change the routine.

## Resistance training

- Currier et al. (2026), ACSM resistance-training position stand — DOI [`10.1249/MSS.0000000000003897`](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000003897).
- Refalo et al. (2023), proximity to failure and hypertrophy — DOI [`10.1007/s40279-022-01784-y`](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-022-01784-y).
- Singer et al. (2024), rest intervals and hypertrophy — DOI [`10.3389/fspor.2024.1429789`](https://doi.org/10.3389/fspor.2024.1429789).
- Wilson et al. (2025), muscle warm-up and force-time outcomes — DOI [`10.1016/j.jshs.2025.101024`](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jshs.2025.101024).
- Haugen et al. (2023), machines versus free weights — DOI [`10.1186/s13102-023-00713-4`](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13102-023-00713-4).

## Concurrent training

- Schumann et al. (2022), concurrent aerobic and strength training — DOI [`10.1007/s40279-021-01587-7`](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-021-01587-7). Cited by the Baye audit's evidence on aerobic claims.

## Training time

- Grgic et al. (2023), time of day for strength and endurance training — DOI [`10.1186/s40798-023-00577-5`](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40798-023-00577-5).

These sources support broad decisions, not uniquely optimal personal parameters. The detailed limitations, conflicts, and claim-by-claim reasoning are in the research audits. A full text in the private pack that is not listed here is not cited by the current documents.
