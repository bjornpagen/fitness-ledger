# Drew Baye Book Claims Catalog

## Scope, reading status, and citation method

This catalog records the meaningful prescriptive, mechanistic, and empirical claims in the seven supplied Baye PDFs before judging them scientifically. It distinguishes a claim that a method *works* from a claim that it is *necessary*, *optimal*, *safer*, or *the greatest possible stimulus*. Short quotations are exact apart from ellipses, capitalization needed to fit a table, and removal of line-break hyphenation. Longer propositions are split rather than fused.

## Comparison with the previous research guide

The prior guide correctly captured Baye's stable emphases on effort, one-set training, controlled repetitions, low frequency, anti-cardio rhetoric, minimalist warm-ups, machines, calorie control, and high protein. It also correctly identified the key analytical error: Baye repeatedly moves from *effective* or *sufficient* to *optimal* or *necessary*.

It needs five corrections:

1. It treated Baye's position as more static than the books show. The 2013/2015 failure wording, 2019 Kratos retractions, and 2025 advanced-method reversals must remain distinct.
2. It did not cite book pages, making it impossible to separate book claims from blog/interview claims.
3. It attributed an “Omega Set”/microtrauma model to the book corpus even though that developed model is not present in the supplied PDFs.
4. It treated the categorical warm-up claim as if Baye allowed no exceptions; the books explicitly allow warm-up for joint symptoms, very heavy methods, and technical rehearsal [Baye, *High Intensity Workouts*, 2nd ed., PDF pp. 67–68, printed pp. 63–64].
5. It did not foreground that the 2025 PPL routines were written around Baye's anabolic-drug-enhanced recovery and therefore are poor defaults for detrained, unsupplemented trainees [Baye, *High Intensity Workouts 2025 Workouts Supplement*, PDF p. 33, printed p. 30].

This catalog is the source layer for the separate evidence audit; verdicts are deliberately withheld here so the extraction is not contaminated by the later scientific judgment.
